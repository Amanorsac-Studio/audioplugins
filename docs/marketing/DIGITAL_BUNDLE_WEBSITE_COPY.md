## 1. The bundle

### Name
**Amanorsac Digital** — ten precision processors.

### One line
Ten precision tools that show you exactly what they are doing to your sound.

### Short paragraph (hero, ~40 words)
Where the analog line adds character, the digital line gives control. Every
plug-in opens on a live picture of your audio: the spectrum, the curve, the
bands, the echoes, the space. Grab what you see and move it. What you hear is
what you drew.

### Long paragraph (about / product page, ~90 words)
Amanorsac Digital is ten separate plug-ins built around one idea: you should
never have to guess. The equalisers draw their curves over the real spectrum,
before and after. The dynamics show each band working as the music plays. The
de-esser and resonance tools draw the change they make, measured from the
audio rather than estimated. The delay shows its echoes travelling, the reverb
shows its tail breathing, the imager shows your stereo field. Every picture is
also a control surface: drag a node, a crossover, a threshold or a tap, and
the panel below follows.

### Three pillars

**See it, grab it.**
Each plug-in is built around a live display fed by the audio itself. Bands,
crossovers, thresholds, focus regions and delay taps are drawn where they act
and can be dragged directly. Click a band in the picture and the control panel
switches to it.

**Measured, not assumed.**
The delay and reverb are tested by measurement: echo timing to the sample, a
decay control that is the real time to fall 60 dB, modulation and time changes
proven free of clicks, freeze proven to hold. Every factory preset in every
product is loaded and run before a build can ship.

**One key, ten plug-ins.**
Buy once, type the key into whichever plug-in you open first, and the whole
digital bundle is licensed on that machine. Two computers per licence, and it
keeps working for thirty days without an internet connection.

**Presets sorted by source.**
120 presets across the bundle, twelve in every plug-in, grouped the same way
as the analog line: Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Strings & Horns,
Mix Bus, Master, Creative and Utility. They ship inside the plug-in.

## 2. Shared feature list (applies to all ten)

- VST3 and Standalone on Windows; VST3, Audio Unit and Standalone on macOS

### PRISM EQ
**Precision Equalizer**

> Twenty-four bands, and every one can live in the middle, the sides, the left or the right.

A precision equaliser you work by hand. The curve and a coloured shape for
each band sit over the live spectrum, input and output together. Drag a node
to move a band, use the wheel for Q, double-click to add or remove one. Any
band can become dynamic, and any band can be placed on Stereo, Left, Right,
Mid or Side.

- Up to 24 bands with eight shapes: bell, low and high shelf, low and high cut, notch, band pass and tilt
- Per-band Stereo, Left, Right, Mid or Side placement, shown as a badge on the node
- Dynamic bands, downward or upward, with the curve moving live as they work; four colour modes from clean to tape

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Mix Bus, Master, Utility
Screenshot: `PRISM EQ.png`

### FLUX DYNAMIC EQ
**Adaptive Dynamic Equalizer**

> An equaliser that only acts when the music needs it.

Up to twelve bands that sit flat until the signal crosses their threshold,
then cut or lift by as much as you allow. The display shows each band's reach
and, in a second curve, what it is doing right now. Adaptive mode follows the
programme level so a setting keeps working as the performance changes.

- Up to 12 dynamic bands, cutting or boosting, each with its own threshold, range, ratio, attack and release
- Adaptive thresholds, auto learn, external sidechain, per-band listen and a delta monitor
- Per-band Stereo, Left, Right, Mid or Side placement

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Mix Bus, Master, Creative, Utility
Screenshot: `FLUX DYNAMIC EQ.png`

### SPECTRA MULTIBAND
**Six-Band Dynamics**

> Split the mix where you want, and watch every band work.

Two to six bands of dynamics over the live spectrum. Drag the crossover lines
to set the split, drag a band's line to set its threshold, and watch a bar in
each band show its gain change as the music plays. Each band can compress,
expand upward or expand downward.

- 2 to 6 bands with draggable crossovers and thresholds, minimum or linear-phase split
- Compress, expand up or expand down per band, with ratio, range, attack, release and makeup
- Per-band solo, bypass and delta, a global link control and a wet/dry mix

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Mix Bus, Master, Creative, Utility
Screenshot: `SPECTRA MULTIBAND.png`

### PULSE LIMITER
**True-Peak Limiter**

> See exactly how hard you are hitting it.

A limiter with a scrolling history: input in grey, output in blue, gain
reduction in red from the top. The ceiling and threshold are lines you drag.
Four styles, from transparent to dense, and a transient control that decides
how much punch survives.

- Draggable threshold and true-peak ceiling over a live input, output and gain reduction history
- Four styles, adaptive or manual release, and lookahead reported to the host
- Transient preserve, stereo link and a delta monitor to hear what is being removed

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Bass, Mix Bus, Master, Creative, Utility
Screenshot: `PULSE LIMITER.png`

### AIR DE-ESSER
**Intelligent De-Essing**

> Point at the ess. Watch it go.

The focus band sits over the live spectrum: drag it to the sibilance, use the
wheel to widen or narrow it, and grab the threshold line to set where it
starts working. A curve shows the reduction actually being applied, measured
from the audio, with the amount in dB.

- Draggable focus, bandwidth and threshold on the spectrum, with measured reduction drawn live
- Wide, split and smart modes, and neutral, bright or dark voice profiles
- Range, attack, release and sensitivity, stereo link, and a listen switch for the detector

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Guitars, Mix Bus, Utility
Screenshot: `AIR DE-ESSER.png`

### RESONANCE CTRL
**Spectral Resonance Controller**

> Takes the ring out and leaves the tone alone.

Tames resonances as they appear, inside a range you set by dragging its edges
on the spectrum. The display draws the change it is making, so you can see
that it is working on the honk and not the body. Learn a source, freeze the
result, or listen to only what is being removed.

- Draggable working range over the live spectrum, with the measured change drawn in real time
- Depth, selectivity, speed, sensitivity and smoothing, plus a control to keep transients
- Learn, freeze and delta, and stereo, mid, side, left or right operation

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Guitars, Keys, Mix Bus, Master, Creative, Utility
Screenshot: `RESONANCE CTRL.png`

### FREQ SHAPER
**Harmonic Spectral Shaper**

> Add harmonics exactly where the track is missing them.

Six zones across the spectrum, each with its own harmonics, density and width.
Drag a zone up for more, drag the lines between zones to move the splits. Put
weight into a bass without touching the top, or air onto a vocal without
touching the chest.

- Six zones with draggable crossovers, each with harmonics, density, width, solo and its own on switch
- A punch control, direct level and wet/dry mix, with auto gain
- The measured change drawn over the live spectrum

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Mix Bus, Master, Creative, Utility
Screenshot: `FREQ SHAPER.png`

### ORBIT DELAY
**Multi-Tap Creative Delay**

> A delay you can see travelling.

A main echo with feedback, plus up to eight taps you place by hand: drag a tap
sideways for time and up and down for level. Each tap has its own pan and
filter. Tempo sync locks everything to the host's sixteenth-note grid, and
changing the time while it plays bends the pitch like a tape echo instead of
clicking.

- A feedback echo plus 8 taps, each with time, level, pan and filter, placed by dragging
- Sync to host tempo, smooth modulation, and ducking that clears the echoes while you play
- Freeze to hold the loop, with pitch-bending time changes and no clicks

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Creative
Screenshot: `ORBIT DELAY.png`

### SPACEVERB PRO
**Algorithmic Reverb**

> Set two seconds. Get two seconds.

A true stereo reverb: early reflections and diffusion feeding an eight-line
network, so tails are dense rather than metallic and a mono source comes back
wide. The decay control is the real time the tail takes to fall 60 dB, from a
tight room to forty seconds. The display shows the tail, the early
reflections and the pre-delay, and you can drag it to change decay and size.

- True stereo with early reflections, pre-delay to 500 ms and decay from 0.1 to 40 seconds
- Size, density, diffusion, early/late balance, and separate low and high damping
- Smooth modulation, ducking, width to 200 % and a freeze that holds the space

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Guitars, Keys, Strings & Horns, Mix Bus, Creative
Screenshot: `SPACEVERB PRO.png`

### WIDE IMAGER
**Frequency-Dependent Stereo**

> Wide where it helps. Mono where it matters.

Five bands of stereo width with a bass-mono control underneath, so the top
can open out while the low end stays solid. A vectorscope and a correlation
meter show what you are doing to the image, and a safe-width switch and a
mono check keep it honest.

- Five width bands from 0 to 250 % with draggable crossovers, plus a bass-mono frequency
- Rotation, mid/side balance and focus
- Live vectorscope and correlation meter, safe width and a one-click mono check

**12 presets** · Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitars, Keys, Mix Bus, Master, Creative, Utility
Screenshot: `WIDE IMAGER.png`

## 7. Words to use, words to avoid

**Use:** precise, live, measured, see, grab, drag, control, clear, exact, true stereo.

**Avoid:** naming any competitor or their products, in page copy, alt text,
meta descriptions or image file names. Do not say "inspired by" or "like" any
other maker's product. Describe what ours does instead.

**Avoid overclaiming:** do not say "zero latency" for the whole bundle (two
products add latency by design), and do not say "linear phase" for PRISM EQ.

## 9. Still needed before the pages go live

- **Price, licence terms and a demo policy.** Only you can supply these.
- **A listening pass on the presets.** They are technically verified, every one loads and runs, but they have not been auditioned by ear.
- **Sample-rate claim.** The digital engines are built for any rate and tested at 48 kHz, with the delay and reverb also measured there. They have not been through a multi-rate audit the way the analog line has, which is why section 5 makes no sample-rate claim. Run one before adding it.
- **Windows code signing.** The Windows installer is unsigned until an Authenticode certificate is added, so SmartScreen warns on first run. Worth fixing before a public launch.
